## The original prototype
An academic project aimed at using genetic programming to move a car from one point to another. The archive contains randomized body and wheel parameters, a candidate population, and terrain generation.

## What remains to build
The original selection and DNA fitness methods are unfinished. A browser adaptation would complete and explain the evaluation, selection, crossover, and mutation cycle rather than present the archive as a finished evolutionary simulation.
