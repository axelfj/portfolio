## Engineering foundations · 2018
A collaborative TEC project simulating an ocean food hierarchy. Fish interact with their environment and with one another through programmed behavioral rules.

## Inside the simulation
- A shared fish abstraction and distinct prey, predator, and superpredator classes.
- Vector-based movement, steering, boundary handling, and a changing flow field.
- Separation, alignment, and cohesion behaviors for groups of fish.
- Hunger, feeding, death, and reproduction within the simulation loop.
- Interactive controls for adding agents and displaying the flow field and perception ranges.

## What it demonstrates
Object-oriented modeling, vector mathematics, and the translation of local behavioral rules into a visual simulation. This is rule-based agent behavior; it is not presented as a machine-learning system.
